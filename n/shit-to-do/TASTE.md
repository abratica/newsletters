# Taste profile: Andrew and Rachel

Read this before picking anything. Andrew updates it by telling Claude in chat; keep edits here, not scattered in the generator.

## Who it's for
- Andrew and his wife Rachel, who live in Somerville, MA. Things the two of them would do together.
- **Greater Boston.** Somerville first, then everywhere nearby: Cambridge, Boston, Medford, Malden, Everett, Arlington, Belmont, Watertown, Waltham, Newton, Brookline, Lexington, Quincy, Saugus and so on. Farther out (North Shore, Worcester, Providence) only if it's special.

## What they're into
- **House and techno as a vibe:** day parties, after-brunch / brunch-into-DJ events, rooftop and patio sets, open-air parties, record fairs. **Not** a calendar of every DJ playing in town. Late-night club nights only when they're a notable house/techno party (e.g. After Brunch events).
- **Free stuff:** always worth including when it's good. Tag it.
- **Movies:** horror, thrillers and dark comedy; special screenings, marathons, repertory and live-score events (Coolidge, Somerville Theatre, Brattle). Andrew has AMC A-List (AMC Assembly Row), so a regular new release isn't an "event" unless it's a special screening.
- **Comedy:** touring stand-up comedians have their own Comedy tab (see GENERATOR.md). Favorite comedians are listed in `watchlist.json` under `comedians`.
- Food and drink events, markets and pop-ups, quirky one-offs, outdoor things in good weather, Survivor or Simpsons themed events if they ever come up.
- Boston sports are covered by another newsletter. Only include sports if it's an unusual event (a fan fest, a watch party, a free open practice).

## Leave out
- Kids' and family-only events, networking and business events, fundraisers and galas, religious services, generic bar trivia and recurring open mics (unless something about it is special), workshops that are really sales pitches.
- DJs who aren't on the watchlist, as standalone listings. A watchlist DJ goes to the DJs in town tab.
